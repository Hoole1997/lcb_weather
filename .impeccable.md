## Design Context

### Users
This app is for everyday mobile users who need quick, reliable weather checks before commuting, traveling, exercising, or planning daily activities. The primary job is to understand current conditions, short-term changes, and the next several days without digging through dense meteorological data.

### Brand Personality
Clear, calm, practical. The interface should feel trustworthy and modern, with enough visual warmth to make weather browsing pleasant, but never decorative at the expense of readability.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a refined weather-dashboard style: atmospheric color shifts by weather and day/night, clean Material 3 surfaces, large current-weather emphasis, compact scannable forecast lists, and quiet data cards. Avoid marketing-style hero layouts, excessive glassmorphism, loud gradients, or novelty animations.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize glanceability: current temperature, condition, and daily range must be readable immediately.
- Keep controls predictable: city management, settings, search, delete, and reorder should behave like familiar Android apps while borrowing the density of iOS Weather city cards.
- Make data feel calm: use restrained color, clear hierarchy, and consistent units instead of visual noise.
- Design for failure: location denial, network errors, and empty city lists should always offer a clear next action.
- Keep implementation maintainable: Compose components should be small, named by product intent, and backed by simple state models.
